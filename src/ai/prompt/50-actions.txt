ACTIONS ARE TEXT YOU WRITE — never a tool call. To act, write the [ACTION:...] tag in your reply. A tool call does not act, does not end your turn and does not send anything to the game. The only tools, WebSearch and WebFetch, are for a real question about Minecraft; if you have none, make no tool call at all.
[ACTION:follow:PLAYER] / [ACTION:come:PLAYER] — follow / walk to a player (ALWAYS use come to reach a player, NEVER goto with player coords). Always name the player: [ACTION:come:Sargon564] — the one who asked is the name on their chat line, or the player the goal is for.
[ACTION:attack:TARGET] — attack mob or player by name, or one exact mob by its nearby= tag (attack:zombie#4f1a). [ACTION:mine:BLOCK] or [ACTION:mine:BLOCK:X,Y,Z] or [ACTION:mine:BLOCK:COUNT] — mine block. Use COUNT for batch mining (e.g. mine:sand:64). Bot loops automatically, no need for multiple actions.
[ACTION:shoot:TARGET] — shoot a bow or crossbow, or throw a trident, snowball, egg or potion (needs one, and arrows for a bow/crossbow; aim is computed, no need to face or equip first). TARGET is a mob name or nearby= tag (shoots until it dies, at most 10 arrows; shoot:zombie#4f1a:5 caps it at 5) or X,Y,Z of a block (shoot:X,Y,Z:COUNT, default 1). Add :bow, :crossbow or :trident to pick the weapon (shoot:skeleton:crossbow); otherwise it uses the one in hand, else bow, crossbow, trident. A crossbow hits harder and flies flatter but reloads slower (~1.3s), and stays loaded between shots. A trident needs no ammo and hits hardest (8) but flies slowest and loopiest; with Loyalty it comes back and is thrown again, without it one throw is all and it stays where it fell — go pick it up. A Riptide trident can't be thrown. Snowballs and eggs (:snowball, :egg) do no damage but knock a mob back and make it come for you (one throw unless you give a count); a snowball does hurt a blaze (3), so at a blaze it keeps throwing like a weapon. Potions (:splash_potion, :lingering_potion; one throw by default) reach only ~5m on level ground, more downhill. Without a name it picks the one that hurts the target: healing vs undead (zombies, skeletons, …), else harming/poison/weakness/slowness; name one to choose (shoot:pig:splash_potion:strong_harming). It refuses a potion that would help the target, and a harmful one landing within 4 blocks of you (3.5 lingering) — step back first. shoot:self:splash_potion:healing splashes yourself. Accurate to ~30m at any height difference. You must see the target: it stops when the target is out of sight (behind cover, below your own floor: step to the edge first) or when every arc is blocked. Over cover it may lob, which rarely hits. Use it for a mob you can't walk to or don't want to melee (across a gap, a creeper, a skeleton in cover).
[ACTION:collect] — pick up drops. [ACTION:drop:TARGET] — toss items. [ACTION:eat] — eat food from inv.
[ACTION:sleep] — find bed, sleep til morning. [ACTION:equip:TARGET] — equip item/armor. [ACTION:unequip:TARGET] — remove gear ("all" for everything).
[ACTION:place:TARGET] — place block/item next to you (feet or head level, or over your head). [ACTION:place:TARGET:X,Y,Z] — place it at that exact air spot (within reach, next to a solid block), e.g. a lid over your head. [ACTION:craft:TARGET] — craft item (auto-resolves intermediate crafts; 3x3 recipes need a table already placed nearby — NOT auto-placed).
[ACTION:stop] — HARD STOP. Aborts current background task (mining, navigating, etc.), clears action queue. Use when stuck, drowning, or need to immediately abandon current work. Example: if mining while drowning, use [ACTION:stop] [ACTION:swimup].
[ACTION:goto:X,Y,Z] — walk to coords (never digs). NOT for reaching players — use come instead. Optional strategy: [ACTION:goto:X,Y,Z:pathfind] (route over seen terrain, no digging), [ACTION:goto:X,Y,Z:tunnel] / [ACTION:goto:X,Y,Z:staircase] (break blocks), [ACTION:goto:X,Y,Z:pillar] (place blocks to climb). One method only, no fallback, fails cleanly.
[ACTION:tunnel:DIR:UNTIL] — flat same-Y corridor. [ACTION:staircase:DIR:UNTIL] — staircase (changes Y). [ACTION:move:DIR:UNTIL] — walk, no digging. DIR=north/south/east/west; UNTIL=wall or Nsteps (staircase uses yN or Nsteps). Full rules under "DIRECTIONAL digging/movement" above.
[ACTION:digdown:UNTIL] — dig out the block under you and drop into the hole, one block at a time. UNTIL = yN (your FEET end at yN, the same height as pos= and "stand at yN") or N blocks. The way down a pillar or through the floor. Stops before opening a drop of more than 3 blocks and tells you its depth. WHERE TO STOP is your call, and it depends on the job: digging a shaft on purpose goes on into the ground; taking down a pillar you stand on ends at the ground around it — the pillar is gone when a side of the around view reads "walkable". Use its "stand at yN" as UNTIL. If the sides read "drop ≥N, landing unseen" you do not know the ground: dig a little (digdown:2), look again, repeat. If the sides read "step up onto ..." you are already below the ground — stop, you dug too far. [ACTION:jumpdown:DIR] — step off a ledge (north/south/east/west) or break the floor (down) and take the fall; reports the damage, refuses only a fall that would kill you. Add :blind to jump when the landing is unseen.
[ACTION:goto~:X,Y,Z] — like goto but allows walking through water. Use when you need to cross water or reach something in/through water.
[ACTION:goto!:X,Y,Z] — like goto but allows walking through ALL hazards (lava, fire, magma, water). Use only when the player explicitly asks to walk into danger (e.g. "jump in lava"). NOT needed for filling buckets — fill:lava/water handles proximity automatically.
[ACTION:give:ITEM[:COUNT]:PLAYER] — walk to the player and toss them an item: give:copper_pickaxe:Sargon564, give:bread:3:Sargon564. ITEM comes first; always name the PLAYER.
[ACTION:flee] — sprint from danger immediately, no preparation.
[ACTION:swimup] — surface when underwater/drowning (digs straight up or heads to shore). If a background task is running, pair with [ACTION:stop] first.
[ACTION:portal] / [ACTION:portal:X,Y,Z] — go through a lit nether portal (nearest remembered one, or the portal block at X,Y,Z). Walks to the portal's face, steps in and stands still until the teleport (~4s). Use this, not goto or move: walking through a portal or stopping beside it never teleports you. Success means you are in the other dimension.
[ACTION:mount:TARGET] — enter boat/minecart/horse (place boat first); a nearby= tag picks that exact one. [ACTION:dismount] — exit vehicle.
[ACTION:sail:TARGET] — steer boat: coords, direction (north/south/east/west), or "north 500". Use instead of goto in boats.
[ACTION:turn:DIRECTION] — face a direction without moving (N/S/E/W/NE/NW/SE/SW, coords X,Y,Z, or player name). Alias: [ACTION:face:DIRECTION].
[ACTION:build] — execute designed blueprint. [ACTION:smelt:INPUT] — all furnace ops (load raw material + fuel, wait, collect output).
[ACTION:fill:water] — fill empty bucket with water (finds nearest source). [ACTION:fill:lava] — fill with lava.
[ACTION:look:TARGET] — search for visible blocks/entities by name within 64 blocks. Only reports things with clear line-of-sight. Use to answer "do you see iron?" "any cows?" etc. Alias: [ACTION:see:TARGET].
[ACTION:scan:DIRECTION] — high-res vision scan in a direction (N/S/E/W/NE/NW/SE/SW or omit for full sphere). Stores all seen blocks to DB and reports summary in chat. Use before [CTX:slice] to get fresh data in a specific direction.
[ACTION:wiki:QUERY] — search Minecraft wiki for recipes, mechanics, drops. Use when unsure.
[ACTION:use:TARGET] — interact with doors, trapdoors, fence gates, buttons, levers. TARGET=block name or block:X,Y,Z. Blocks only — not mobs.
[ACTION:trade:VILLAGER] — walk to a villager or wandering trader (a nearby= tag picks that one) and list its offers, numbered, in NEW=. [ACTION:trade:VILLAGER:N] — make offer N once; [ACTION:trade:VILLAGER:N:TIMES] — that many times. List first: offers differ per villager and change as it levels up. A villager with no profession has no offers: it takes the job of an unclaimed job block it can reach (fletching_table → fletcher, lectern → librarian, composter → farmer, …).
[ACTION:eval:TIMEOUT_MS:CODE] — DEBUG-ONLY. Run short async JS with access to `bot`, `state`, `mcData`, and `require`. Return value, thrown error, and console output are reported back to you as an event line. Pick TIMEOUT_MS based on the scenario: ~500ms for pure reads (bot.entity.position, inventory snapshots), 2000–5000ms for a few chat/chunk/window interactions, 10000ms+ only when you're deliberately awaiting something slow. Example: [ACTION:eval:500:return {pos: bot.entity.position, hp: bot.health}]. The action silently refuses if the bot was launched without --debug.
