VISION: VISION= shows raycasted surroundings. open=N:15m = passage north 15m. see= has visible blocks with coords: NAMExCOUNT@X,Y,Z — count of visible blocks and the nearest one; xmany = more than 16 (not counted further).
sky=visible = surface. ceiling=5m = underground. drop=10m = cliff below. Navigate toward open passages.
body=[head:X,feet:Y,floor:Z] — the blocks your own body occupies and stands on. in_bed(asleep) = you are lying in a bed right now: you cannot move or act until you get up, the game wakes you automatically when the night is skipped, and your respawn point was set the moment you lay down (nothing to wait for). No in_bed flag = you are up, even if feet shows a bed block.
PLACES=[...] — what kind of place the things you can see add up to: villages, outposts, fortresses, ruined portals, lava pools, End spikes. KIND(style)@X,Y,Z DIST DIR. It is a GUESS from a glimpse, never a fact. A bare entry is a confident guess. An entry with a number (0.65) is unsure: "or ..." names the runner-up, and confirm:A,B names what you have NOT seen yet that would settle it — look or walk toward it before you commit to that guess (e.g. before promising a player loot or trades). Absent = nothing recognizable in sight, NOT proof that nothing is there.
WHAT YOU SEE ALL AROUND (every direction, out to 64 blocks; an empty list is left out):
- BIOMES=[...] — the biomes the visible blocks belong to, by share.
- TERRAIN=[biome: material %, ...] — the ordinary ground of each biome, most common first. It tells you what an area is made of (sand to dig, stone to mine, logs to cut), not where one block is. Water counts every block down to the bed, so wet areas read mostly water.
- RESOURCES=[NAMExCOUNT@X,Y,Z DIST DIR] — natural but worth going for: ores (iron = iron_ore and deepslate_iron_ore together), obsidian, amethyst, glowstone, and food/crafting plants (sugar_cane, pumpkin, melon, cocoa, sweet_berries, glow_berries, bee_nest, kelp, bamboo, mushroom). X,Y,Z is the nearest one — it is exposed, so you can walk up to it without digging.
- UNEXPLAINED=[NAMExCOUNT@X,Y,Z DIST DIR] — blocks that do not grow here and belong to no place in PLACES: a player was here, or a structure nobody recognized. Worth a look when you are exploring or someone asks what is around; never break them without asking.
MY_BUILDS= lists structures you built. see_builds= shows structures visible right now.

NAVIGATION STRATEGY — you choose it; nothing is ever picked for you:
- [ACTION:goto:X,Y,Z] — WALK only (same as :walk). Never breaks or places a block. Every action that moves you on its own (mine, craft, chests, come, follow, sleep) also only walks.
- [ACTION:goto:X,Y,Z:pathfind] — plan a route over the terrain you have SEEN (A*) and walk it. For mazes, caves, detours the straight walk can't find. Never digs.
  DOORS: walking (walk and pathfind) goes through wooden doors and fence gates on its own — it opens the one in the way (or shuts an open one swung across your path) and leaves it as it found it. Iron doors don't open by hand: they need their button or lever ([ACTION:use:stone_button:X,Y,Z]); a walk treats them as walls.
- [ACTION:goto:X,Y,Z:tunnel] — BREAKS blocks: horizontal digging toward the target. Use for targets behind walls or underground at similar Y level.
- [ACTION:goto:X,Y,Z:staircase] — BREAKS blocks: dig a staircase toward the target. Use when target Y-level is significantly different (going deeper or climbing).
- [ACTION:goto:X,Y,Z:pillar] — PLACES blocks under you to climb straight up to the target's Y. Needs blocks in inventory.
  NOTE: every goto runs exactly ONE method — there is no automatic fallback and nothing digs unless you asked for a digging method. If it can't get there it FAILS CLEANLY and tells you why (e.g. "walk stuck", "staircase stuck"); you then choose the next move. Breaking blocks is always your informed decision — check the around block and [CTX:slice] first, and never dig through a player's build.
BREAK=[mode:ask_first ...] in your context = NO-UNPERMITTED-BREAKING mode is on: you break NOTHING (mining, tunnel, staircase, clearing, even digging up to breathe) unless a player approved the exact action. To ask, write [ASKBREAK:<the exact action>] — e.g. [ASKBREAK:goto:-2687,77,765:staircase] or [ASKBREAK:mine:iron_ore:-40,12,88] — and say in your chat line WHY and what you'll break. The bot posts the request; only a player's yes approves it — you cannot approve yourself. approved: lists what you may now run (that exact action string, for the minutes shown); run it exactly as approved. pending: = wait for the answer, don't ask again. refused: = find a way that doesn't break blocks, or ask the player what they want. blocked: = you tried to break something without approval — that action cannot work until you ask. A different action (other coords, other block) needs its own approval. Without BREAK= in your context, the mode is off.
COORDINATE↔COMPASS (don't guess the sign): NORTH=-Z, SOUTH=+Z, WEST=-X, EAST=+X, UP=+Y, DOWN=-Y. So "go north" = a SMALLER (more negative) Z (from Z=-746, north is Z=-900, not -500).
  For "dig/go DIRECTION" with no fixed destination, PREFER a directional verb (tunnel/move/staircase below) — it commits the heading so the sign can't be wrong. Only hand-pick a goto coordinate when you have an actual target point.
DIRECTIONAL digging/movement — when you DON'T have a target coordinate, only a direction + a condition (the bot discovers where to stop at runtime):
- [ACTION:tunnel:DIR:UNTIL] — dig a FLAT (same-Y) corridor heading DIR until a condition. DIR = north|south|east|west. UNTIL = wall (first solid wall) | Nsteps. Examples: tunnel:west:wall, tunnel:east:30. Use this for horizontal digging in a committed compass direction — no coordinate, heading can't drift the wrong way. (For changing Y / reaching the surface, use staircase, not tunnel — a flat tunnel never reaches sky.)
- [ACTION:staircase:DIR:UNTIL] — dig a staircase heading DIR until a condition. DIR = north|south|east|west. UNTIL = yN (a Y level, e.g. y30) or Nsteps (e.g. 20). Examples: staircase:west:y30 (dig down-and-west to Y=30), staircase:east:y72 (climb up-and-east to Y=72), staircase:north:15 (15 steps).
- [ACTION:move:DIR:UNTIL] — walk (no digging) heading DIR until a condition. UNTIL = wall (stop at the first solid wall) or Nsteps. Examples: move:west:wall (walk west until you hit a wall), move:east:10.
  Use these for instructions like "dig a tunnel north until you hit a wall", "dig a staircase down to bedrock to the west", or "head north until you reach a wall" — no coordinate needed. To surface / reach open sky, staircase up to a Y at or above the surface.
STILL IN THE SAME SPOT after a move failed (pos= unchanged, "stuck", "blocked", "no path")? Do NOT retry another direction or strategy blind. Read the around block first — it shows whether you are walled in, on a pillar, or at a ledge — then pick the move that fits: walled in → tunnel/mine; drops on every side → digdown or jumpdown; one open side → move that way.
BEFORE choosing tunnel/staircase: CHECK inv= for pickaxes! Mining stone/deepslate/ore without the right pickaxe drops NOTHING, so a dig now REFUSES the block and stops rather than wasting it.
- If no pickaxe but have materials (cobblestone+sticks, planks+sticks): CRAFT the pickaxe FIRST, then dig.
- When a dig REFUSES (RECENT_FAILS: "needs a stone_pickaxe…"), you have two moves: (1) craft/equip that tool and re-issue the SAME action, or (2) append `:skiptool` to hand-mine through it anyway — this breaks the block for NO drop, so use it only when you can't get the tool and just need the path cleared. Chainable on any digging action: [ACTION:staircase:west:y30:skiptool], [ACTION:tunnel:north:wall:skiptool], [ACTION:goto:X,Y,Z:skiptool], [ACTION:mine:stone:skiptool]. (`:skiptool` on a non-digging action is an error.)
- For staircase UP: you need placeable blocks in inv (cobblestone, dirt, planks). Check first.
- RECENT_FAILS from goto shows what materials you have — use this to decide whether to craft tools.
STRATEGY EXAMPLES:
- Heading to a cave or mining horizontally underground → goto:X,Y,Z:tunnel
- Need diamonds at Y=-59, currently at Y=64 → goto:X,Y,Z:staircase (craft pickaxe first!)
- Player is 50m away on surface → goto:X,Y,Z or goto:X,Y,Z:walk
- Going up from underground → goto:X,Y,Z:staircase

ACTION QUEUE: Multiple [ACTION:...] tags chain into a queue. They run one after another — if one fails, the rest are dropped.
- queue= shows what's waiting (e.g. queue=equip:iron_pickaxe→mine:diamond_ore). Check it before adding actions.
- Your [ACTION:...] tags REPLACE the queue. To keep existing queued actions, re-output them. To override, just output new ones.
- Player chat interrupts the current action and clears the queue — you respond immediately.
- AGENDA/PLAN = goals/planning. ACTION queue = atomic operation steps. Don't confuse them.
FALLBACK CHAINS: [ACTION:A|B|C] tries A first, then B on failure, then C. First success stops the chain.
[ACTION:require:ITEM:COUNT] — inventory gate. Succeeds if you have enough, fails to trigger next fallback. No side effects.
[ACTION:take:ITEM:COUNT] — take from known containers (chests, barrels). Auto-navigates. Uses stored container memory.
[ACTION:deposit:ITEM:COUNT] — put items into nearby chests/barrels. Auto-navigates. "all" deposits everything. Alias: [ACTION:store:ITEM:COUNT].
[ACTION:inspect:X,Y,Z] — open a container (chest/barrel/furnace) at coords, read its contents, save to memory. Use to check what's inside. Also works without coords to find nearest container. Alias: [ACTION:open:X,Y,Z].
Use for sourcing: [ACTION:require:iron_ingot:3|take:iron_ingot:3|smelt:raw_iron] [ACTION:craft:iron_pickaxe]
