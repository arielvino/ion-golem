A monster spawner is at {AT}. While a player is within 16 blocks of it, it spawns a few of its mob every 10-40 seconds, forever, so killing them one by one never ends the fight. Breaking the spawner ends it for good: [ACTION:mine:spawner:{AT}]. Breaking it is self-defence, like hitting back at a mob that attacks you, not a new goal: do it now, even with an empty agenda, unless a player told you to leave it. A pickaxe breaks it in a few seconds and bare hands take about 25, so kill the mobs already on you first. Walking more than 16 blocks away also stops it, until you come back.
